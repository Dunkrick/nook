# Workspace

The workspace is the product; everything else is furniture. The canvas remains quiet, the wall gives thoughts an infinite-feeling home, and the toolbar floats without competing with content.

## Rules

- The outer canvas is neutral and low contrast.
- The wall may use a restrained dot texture to establish scale.
- Artifacts own the strongest color and contrast.
- Floating controls use translucent artifact material and overlay elevation.
- Keep the world larger than the viewport so the desk reads as open space.

The current implementation is intentionally camera-static. DS-1 does not add panning, zooming, or behavior changes.
