# Logo

The Nook wordmark is Plus Jakarta Sans Bold with a compact coral diamond spark. The spark is a small signal of a thought arriving, never decoration for its own sake.

## Family

- **Wordmark:** `Nook` plus spark; default toolbar mark.
- **Monogram:** `N` with the same spark construction; compact navigation use.
- **Spark:** standalone only for loading or a small moment of creation.
- **App icon:** monogram on an iris field.

Keep clear space equal to the spark width around every mark. Use iris on light materials or cream on dark materials. Do not recolor, stretch, outline, or animate the wordmark continuously. Any future loading animation should be one calm 180–350ms spark arrival.
