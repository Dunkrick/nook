# Brand

## Thesis

Nook is a personal thinking desk. It gives small ideas room to land, move, and connect.

## Personality

Calm, warm, and quietly capable. Playful only when the work invites it. Never noisy, corporate, skeuomorphic, or precious.

## Voice

Use direct, generous language: “Everything starts with one thought.” Prefer invitations over instructions and concrete verbs over productivity jargon. Name user content “thoughts,” not tasks, tickets, or objects.

## Naming

The workspace is the **desk** or **wall** in product copy. User-created units are **thoughts**. Temporary input is a **draft**. A collection selected for reflection is a **selection**.
