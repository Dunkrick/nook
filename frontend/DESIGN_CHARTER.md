# Nook Design Charter

Nook is a personal creative-studio wall for thinking spatially. Its foundation is editorial clarity; its personality comes from color, material, and small moments of surprise.

## Design principles

1. **Make the thought the hero.** Controls support the work and never compete with it.
2. **Let the wall feel physical.** Paper, depth, outlines, rotation, and shadows communicate a place where ideas can land.
3. **Earn the playful moments.** Color and motion should confirm an action, reveal state, or create orientation—not decorate empty space.
4. **Keep interaction obvious.** Hover, focus, drag, edit, draft, and selected states must be distinct without relying on color alone.
5. **Protect spatial thinking.** A thought’s position is meaningful. Future camera behavior must preserve that relationship.

## UI review checklist

- Is the purpose clear before any decorative treatment?
- Does this belong on a warm, personal creative wall?
- Does the motion or color clarify a state or provide a useful reward?
- Is the state usable with keyboard focus and readable at a glance?
- Does the change preserve the user’s spatial relationship with their thoughts?
