Logo system

05 / LOGO
01

Custom N

A hand-set N, not a typeface glyph — the diagonal bows very slightly rather than running straight.

02

Slightly open O

The right stroke resolves into a ring that never fully closes — a nook is a space you can still enter.

03

Tiny spark

A small, detached mark above the O. The moment a thought becomes clear.

04

Subtle asymmetry

Unequal stroke lengths and an off-axis spark keep the mark from feeling manufactured.
