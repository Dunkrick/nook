# Product thesis
**Important things deserve to stay visible**

## Mission
Help people create a personal space where meaningful things remain in sight instead of disappearing into folders, notes, or screenshots.

## Vision
Build the most delightful digital wall on the internet.

## Personality
1. Human
2. Playful
3. Calm

## What we are not:
- Notion
- Trello
- Pinterest
- Google keep
- Obsidian
- Whiteboard software

### Design Manifesto
1. Interaction over Interface
2. Imperfection over Precision
3. Visibility over Storage
4. Creation over Configuration
5. Simplicity over Features
