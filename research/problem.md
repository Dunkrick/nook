# Current Observation

People already create physical walls using:

- Sticky notes
- Quotes
- Photos
- Drawings
- Tickets
- Notes
- Reminders

These walls exist because people want important things to remain visible instead of buried inside apps.

# Current User Behaviour

Today people use:

- Apple Notes
- WhatsApp Saved Messages
- Google Keep
- Screenshots
- Notion/Evernote/Obsidian
- Pinterest
- Bookmarks
- Physical walls
- Whiteboards

# Current Problems

- Things become buried.
- I forget why I saved something.
- Everything is scattered.
- Important ideas disappear.
- Physical walls aren't portable.
- Sometimes I feel like I need privacy for few things, but they still gotta be put on the wall.
